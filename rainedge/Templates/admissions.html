Application Process

Four simple steps to start your journey with us

Submit Application

Submit your learner's application through our website.

2-3 days

Document Review

We will receive your application and our team will review it and get back to you at their earliest convenience.

5-7 days

Assessment Interview

An interview will be conducted with your learner present.This will occur either digitally or physically.

1-2 weeks

Final Decision

The body responsible for your learner's admission wil then make a decision on whether or not your learner is elegible.

2-3 weeks

Admission Requirements

Academic Transcripts

Official high school, primary or college records

Standardized Tests

SAT, ACT, or equivalent scores(For college students)

Personal Statement

500-800 word essay on who you are and your goals

Recommendation Letters

Two letters from academic references

Portfolio (where applicable)

For art, design, and creative programs

Application Fee

Ksh.2,500 processing fee (waived for qualified students)

International Students

Additional requirements include English proficiency scores (TOEFL/IELTS) and visa documentation.
