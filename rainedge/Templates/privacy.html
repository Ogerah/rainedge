Privacy Policy

At Rainedge.Intl, we are committed to protecting the privacy and security of our students, parents/guardians, staff and visitors. This policy explains how we collect, use, store and share personal information, and your rights in relation to that information.

  1. Home
  2. Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2025

1. Information We Collect

We collect and process personal data about individuals in our school community, including prospective, current and former students, parents/guardians, staff, volunteers and visitors. The types of personal data we collect include:

1.1 Identity & Contact Data

  • Full name, date of birth, gender
  • Address, telephone number, email address
  • Parent/guardian names, emergency contacts

1.2 Educational, Health & Support Data

  • Student records (enrolment, attendance, grades, assessments)
  • Health or medical information including allergies, special educational needs, dietary requirements
  • Behavioural records, pastoral care information

1.3 Technology & Usage Data

When you use our website, learning platforms or network, we may collect: IP address, log-in data, device type, browsing activities, cookies. We explain our cookie usage later in this policy.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use personal data for lawful educational operations, including but not limited to:

  • Providing education, teaching, learning and support services
  • Managing student enrolment, admissions, progression and alumni relations
  • Ensuring student health, wellbeing, safeguarding and welfare
  • Communicating with parents/guardians, staff and external parties
  • Maintaining our IT and infrastructure to deliver educational services securely
  • Meeting legal and regulatory obligations (e.g., data reporting, inspections)

3. Information Sharing & Disclosure

We will not sell your personal data. We may share personal data with:

  • Anybody you have provided consent for
  • Third-party service providers working on our behalf (for example, IT services, learning platform providers) under strict contract terms
  • Regulatory authorities or bodies as required by law (e.g., education authorities, child protection agencies)

We take steps to ensure any third party processing your data does so securely and only for the purposes we specify.

4. Data Security & Storage

We employ appropriate technical and organisational safeguards to protect your data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or destruction. These include:

Encryption & Access Controls

Our systems use encrypted connections, secure user authentication and role-based access rights.

Secure Storage & Retention

Information is stored in secure facilities or cloud services and retained only for as long as needed, then securely deleted or anonymised.

5. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the following rights in relation to your personal data:

Right to Access

You may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.

Right to Rectification

If you believe any personal data is inaccurate or incomplete, you may ask us to correct or update it.

Right to Erasure

You may request that we erase your personal data under certain conditions.

Other Rights

You may also have rights to restrict processing, data portability, object to processing, or complain to a supervisory authority.

6. Cookies & Tracking

When you visit our website or use our digital services, we use cookies and similar tracking technologies to provide a better user experience and to analyse website usage. You can control cookies via your browser or via the cookie banner on our site.

Cookie Type Purpose Duration
Essential Required for site navigation and security Session
Analytics Helps analyse site usage to improve services 2 years
Marketing Tracks preferences to show relevant content 1 year

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do so, we will publish the new version on our website with the updated “Last updated” date.
