Application Process

Four simple steps to start your journey with us

Submit Application

Submit your application through our website.

2-3 days

Document Review

We will receive your application and our team will review it and get back to you at their earliest convenience.

5-7 days

Assessment Interview

An interview will be conducted with you present.This will occur either digitally or physically.

1-2 weeks

Final Decision

The body responsible for your admission wil then make a decision on whether or not you are elegible.

2-3 weeks
