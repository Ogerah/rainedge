A little More About Us

Rainedge International is a Christian non-profit organization located in Nakuru, Kenya, dedicated to the important work of alleviating poverty. The organization focuses its efforts on marginalized children and vulnerable youth within the community, with a special emphasis on helping street children find a better life. Committed to its Christian foundation, Rainedge International operates on the principles of compassion and service, offering its assistance to those in need without regard to their religious beliefs, gender, or ethnic background. This commitment to inclusivity ensures that help is available to all who require it, embodying the Christian values that guide the organization's mission. The name "Rainedge" itself carries significant meaning, symbolizing the hope and promise of an end to hardship and difficult times. This name is particularly relevant in the Kenyan context, where the country's agricultural and overall well-being are deeply dependent on rainfall. Just as rain brings life and prosperity to the land, Rainedge International strives to bring renewed hope and opportunities to the lives of the children and youth it serves, marking a turning point where hardship gives way to a brighter future.

Our Story

Driven by a profound sense of compassion and a desire to alleviate suffering, Sammy Omufwoko, the director of Rainedge International, embarked on a life-changing journey in 2006. His inspiration stemmed from witnessing firsthand the immense hardships endured by vulnerable young boys living on the streets of Nakuru. Deeply moved by their plight, he resolved to dedicate his efforts to supporting the poor, orphans, and marginalized members of the community. Omufwoko's initial response was to launch a feeding program specifically designed to provide nourishment to impoverished street families. This initiative served as a vital lifeline for those struggling to meet their basic needs. However, his commitment deepened further when several young boys, seeking refuge and a sense of belonging, followed him back to his humble abode. Discovering them sheltering in his makeshift house ignited a new vision within him. Inspired by this encounter and recognizing the urgent need for a safe haven, Omufwoko made the decision to establish an orphanage. He began by taking in four boys, providing them with shelter, food, and care. As the organization grew, he observed an increasing number of girls facing similar challenges and vulnerabilities. Recognizing the equal importance of their well-being, he extended the orphanage's mission to include girls, ensuring that both boys and girls had access to the support and opportunities they deserved.
